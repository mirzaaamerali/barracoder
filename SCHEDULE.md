## How the season maps

The season runs in four phases across Tue/Thu 90-minute meetings. Phase 1 — Foundation (Aug 13 – Aug 27, 5 meetings): TMG Sessions 1-4 at roughly one session per meeting (Session 1 split across two for the full 15-model field build), ending with the locked problem statement (Session 3 deadline) and Checkpoint 1. Phase 2 — Build & Iterate (Sep 1 – Oct 1, 8 meetings): the double-page Sessions 5-8 each split across two meetings (project track Tue, robot track Thu), Checkpoint 2 rubric handout after Session 8, then Session 9 plus a dedicated rules-mastery/launch-drill meeting reflecting the coach's rules priority. Phase 3 — Competition Prep (Oct 6 – Nov 5, 8 meetings): Sessions 10-12 split across six meetings (presentations built, first timed 2:30 matches, dress rehearsal), then FOUR dedicated scrimmage-rehearsal meetings (Oct 27, Oct 29, Nov 3, Nov 5) covering full timed matches with precision tokens and the scoring calculator, mock equipment inspection and pit/setup logistics, and full judging run-throughs — all paperwork closes Oct 31, ahead of the Nov 8 Sunday scrimmage treated as a real qualifier. Phase 4 — Debrief, Peak & Wrap (Nov 10 – Dec 17, 11 meetings): scrimmage results converted into a prioritized robot/mission and judging improvement backlog, alternating iteration and rehearsal through Thanksgiving, a Challenge Updates recheck and full dress rehearsals peaking for the December main event (date TBD — Dec 8/10 meetings flex around it), and celebration/disassembly/inventory wrap-up on Dec 15 and 17. All four equally weighted judged areas (Robot Game, Robot Design, Innovation Project, Core Values) get continuous coverage: table time appears in nearly every meeting once the driving base exists (Aug 20), the innovation project deliverables track the TMG's dated worksheet milestones, and every meeting keeps the official rhythm of intro/Core Value, dual-track tasks, reflection, share, and clean-up.
